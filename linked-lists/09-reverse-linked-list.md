
## Bonus Problem: Reverse Linked List (Easy)

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/reverse-linked-list/

### Approach
I used an iterative three-pointer technique. The previous, current, and next pointers are used to reverse each node's link one by one until the entire linked list is reversed.

### Complexity
- Time: O(n)
- Space: O(1)

### Notes
The solution reverses the linked list in place without creating another linked list.
